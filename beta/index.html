The profit formula (beta partner)

Profit = sales − refunds − (product + shipping) − ad spend

SalesShopify order totals for the month, before tax
RefundsShopify refunds issued that month
Product + shippingwhat your supplier (CJ) charged for those orders
Ad spendGoogle Ads cost for the month
Example month$6,000 sales − $200 refunds − $1,500 product − $3,000 ads = $1,300 profit → you pay $130. A month at zero or below: you pay $0, and nothing carries over.
