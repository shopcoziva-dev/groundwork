Step 1 of 3 · pick your price
The profit formula

Profit = sales − refunds − (product + shipping) − ad spend

Example month: $6,000 sales − $200 refunds − $1,500 product − $3,000 ads = $1,300 profit → you pay $130. A month at zero or below: you pay $0, nothing carries over.
