The rule that runs everything

Price near the top, never below the floor

Price = the lower of
.99 up( M × k ) and .99 down( H )
keep it only if Price ≥ F
  • M what shoppers already pay in the ads
  • k the markup: 2.0× cheap, 1.4× expensive
  • H the market's top: never above it
  • F the floor: keep $20 and 25%, after ads
